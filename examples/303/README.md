# 303: DYCOMS-II RF01 stratocumulus (Stevens et al. 2005)

Full-size validation case for the DYCOMS free-troposphere radiation (case hook `dycoms_rf01`), the liquid-water
longwave scheme, the Monin-Obukhov facet surface and the bldump diagnostics (LWP, cloud cover, `zi_qt` with `zi_qt_thres = 8e-3`).
Inputs are generated by `tools/python/examples/dales_cases/make_rf01.py` (`python make_rf01.py examples/303`).
Run with `tools/hpc_execute.sh examples/303` (32 ranks, 8 x 4); compare with `compare_dales.py` (entrainment
rate from `zi_qt` with D = 3.75e-6 1/s). The DALES reference inputs are in
`tools/python/examples/dales_cases/dales_inputs/rf01` (DALES has no RF01 case of its own); results in RESULTS.md.

## Floor

The floor is one horizontal facet covering the domain (`libm = .true.`, `lbottom = .false.`, `nfcts = 1`),
as in every uDALES case: the flat bottom boundary (`lbottom`) is not used. Its IBM files (`facets.inp`,
`factypes.inp`, `facetarea.inp`, `solid_*.txt`, `fluid_boundary_*.txt`, `facet_sections_*.txt`) were written by
`python -m udprep.flat_floor examples/303 303 --itot 96 --jtot 96 --dx 35 --dy 35 --z1 2.5 --z0 1.445944e-5 --z0h 1.445944e-5`
(from `tools/python`), which prints the `&WALLS` counts. The surface layer is the Monin-Obukhov facet scheme
(`iwallmom = 4`, the DALES surface with a local Obukhov length per facet section). The floor's facet type (-1)
has `lGR = 1` in `factypes.inp.303`: in uDALES this green flag only means that the facet exchanges
moisture (`iwallmoist = 1` applies `bcqfz` to it).

## Time step

The adaptive settings were first matched to an earlier DALES run and to uDALES with the
e12 halo bug (#405, since fixed). With the fix the uDALES
step is 0.47-0.50 s against DALES 0.40-0.43 s (hourly means), so the adaptive pair is no longer matched; the
close-match comparison uses the fixed step below. All DALES steps quoted here come from DALES runs without
cloud-top longwave cooling (before the `iradiation = 10` fix, RESULTS.md) and have not been re-measured against the
corrected DALES; the adaptive pair is secondary. The earlier DALES run was limited by diffusion (`peclet = 0.15`, 32368 of 35236 steps), not by its Courant number (L2, 0.1-0.14 at `courant = 0.5`):

| window | DALES (invalid run) | uDALES `diffnr = 0.16` | uDALES `diffnr = 0.25` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-10 min | 0.485 s | 0.468 s | 0.731 s |
| 10-20 min | 0.523 s | 0.502 s | 0.738 s |
| 20-23 min | 0.435 s | 0.437 s | (0.600 s, 20-30 min) |

`diffnr = 0.16` is DALES `peclet = 0.15` times 1.041, rounded up (`min(dz, dx)^2 (1/dz^2 + 1/dx^2 + 1/dy^2)` for dz = 5 m, dx = 35 m); with
it the step is within 5 % of DALES. A run with `courant = 1.0` gave the same series, so `courant` is not limiting. These
numbers come from 30 min runs (1260-1800 s were reached), before the cloud develops; later in the run the step will
differ. For a **fixed** step, uncomment the two commented lines in `&RUN` of `namoptions.303` (`ladaptive = .false.`,
`dtmax = 0.3`): `courant` and `diffnr` are then ignored, and `runtime` must be a multiple of `dtmax`. 0.3 s is below the
adaptive step of both codes (DALES hourly means 0.40-0.43 s) and divides the 60 s output interval; it is the step of the
close-match comparison in RESULTS.md (DALES inputs in `tools/python/examples/dales_cases/dales_inputs/rf01`).

## RF01 z0 value

`z0 = z0h = 1.445944e-5 m`. With `iwallmom = 4` uDALES computes `Cm = kappa^2 / ln(z1/z0)^2` (stability
corrections included) at `z1 = 2.5 m`; the value above gives exactly Cm = 0.0011 for neutral conditions
(printed and checked by `make_rf01.py`). In the unstable boundary layer of RF01 the effective Cm is larger than
0.0011 because of the stability correction (the first 60 s of the smoke test gave u* = 0.29 m/s, about
sqrt(0.0011) x 8.9 m/s). PyCLES uses a constant Cm = 0.0011, so u* here is slightly higher.

## Known property: top-cell cooling

With uDALES's zero-flux top boundary for thl, the DYCOMS free-troposphere radiative cooling is not balanced
by subsidence warming in the topmost cell, which therefore cools by about 0.2 K over 4 h. That cell lies inside
the sponge layer (the sponge starts at about 1125 m, the top cell is centred at 1497.5 m), so it should not
reach the boundary layer or the cloud. It is documented here; no correction is applied.

## Deviations from DALES/PyCLES

- uDALES has no 5th-order advection and no WENO scheme (PyCLES uses WENO); central differences for qt are
  oscillatory at the 840 m inversion. Stevens et al. 2005 note that many models lose the cloud for numerical
  reasons; a low LWP should be diagnosed (advection, SGS) before it is counted as a port bug.
- The SGS closure (one-equation TKE) differs from the models of the intercomparison.
- Noise follows DALES (thl 0.1 K and qt 2.5e-5 kg/kg at all levels, no u, v, w noise); PyCLES perturbs +-0.05 K below 200 m only.
- Surface drag by an equivalent `z0` (see above); heat and moisture fluxes are fixed.
- Constant latent heat `rlv = 2.53e6` J/kg (the DALES value) in the surface moisture flux; PyCLES uses a
  temperature-dependent L.

## Concerns

- RF01 is sensitive to numerics (see above) and to the sharp inversion between the cells at 837.5 m and 842.5 m.
- The reference `thls`, `qts` and the initial `e12` are choices, not paper values.
